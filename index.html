Welcome to SUU Baja Racing

SUU Baja Racing is a social and engineering group at Southern Utah University that designs, builds, and races an off-road baja style vehicle once a year at a national SAE organized competition.

Who We Are

Members are current students of the university who dedicate their time to learning the skills necessary to design and build a vehicle meeting SAE requirements. Students not only learn CAD modeling and shop skills, but find a place where they can meet students with common interests, degrees, and goals.

Team Photo Placeholder

What We Do

SUU Baja Racing meets every Tuesday and Thursday to design, build, and test a baja style vehicle. During meetings, team members learn CAD modeling skills, fabrication skills, and effective teamwork strategies. Fabrication skills include basic metal working, welding, plasma cutting, and more.

Members are given tasks to accomplish on the vehicle, determined by what needs to be completed to meet the set deadlines. Team members are encouraged to ask questions and learn from each other.

The SAE Competition

The completed vehicle will travel to a national SAE organized event, in which the team competes against 100 other universities. At this event, the team participates in several challenges including:

Acceleration Test

Testing vehicle speed and drivetrain response.

Hill Climb

Evaluating torque and incline traction limits.

Suspension & Travel

Navigating rough terrain and high drops.

Maneuverability

Testing steering precision and agility.

The Main Event

4-Hour Endurance Race

Held on a custom-made track on the last day of competition. Drivers race wheel to wheel on challenging terrain, competing to see who can make it the furthest.
